AUTOCLIPPER

THE BEST STREAM MOMENTS, EDITED FOR VERTICAL VIDEO

AutoClipper is a personal tool. Several times a day it finds the most-clipped moments from public Twitch streams, edits one of them into a vertical video (subtitles, title, streamer credit) and sends it to the owner's own TikTok account as a draft.

Nothing is published automatically: the owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, adds the caption and decides whether to post it.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit: the owner signs in once with TikTok to authorize the tool.
  • Content Posting API (upload): each edited video is uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (scope video.upload).
  • user.info.basic: used only to confirm which account is connected.

Who can use it

AutoClipper is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own TikTok account.
