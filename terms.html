1. What AutoClipper is

AutoClipper is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its owner. It edits short highlight videos from public Twitch clips and uploads them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Use of TikTok

AutoClipper uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only to upload videos to the connected account's drafts. It never publishes, comments, likes, follows or interacts with other accounts. Every video is reviewed by the owner in the TikTok app before any publication. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content and credit

Clips come from public Twitch broadcasts. Each video credits the original streamer on screen and in the description. A streamer who wants a video removed can ask through the contact below, and it will be removed.
