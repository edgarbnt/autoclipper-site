AUTOCLIPPER

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who is concerned

AutoClipper is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not have other users, does not offer accounts and does not collect personal data from visitors of this website.

2. TikTok data

When the owner connects their TikTok account, AutoClipper receives an access token and a refresh token from TikTok, and the basic account information allowed by the user.info.basic scope. They are used only to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok drafts. Tokens are stored encrypted (as secrets of the owner's private GitHub repository) and are never shared with anyone.

3. Twitch data

AutoClipper reads public information from the Twitch API (public clips, stream titles, viewer counts) to choose and edit highlight videos. It does not collect data about Twitch viewers.

4. Sharing and retention

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Working files are deleted after each run, except a list of already used clips, kept to avoid duplicates. The owner can revoke TikTok access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services).
